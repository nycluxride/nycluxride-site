The sequence

What happens after you send the details

Dispatch answers every request from a person, not an automatic queue.

Read the full FAQ

  1. Send the details. Use the booking page, call, or send the inquiry form below. Have ready the pickup date and time, the pickup and drop-off points, the number of passengers and bags, a vehicle class, and a name and contact number.

  2. Dispatch replies in writing. A person checks a car and a chauffeur against the time requested, then answers by email or WhatsApp. A request is not a held reservation until that reply arrives, so wait for it before building a schedule around the pickup.

  3. The rate is confirmed before the day. The reply names the class of car, the base rate, and any fee that applies, such as meet and greet inside an airport terminal. Base rates are quoted separately from tolls and a standard gratuity. The gratuity is charged at booking with the fare, and tolls, airport parking, taxes and government trip charges are charged after the ride to the card saved at booking, each with an itemized receipt.

  4. The arrival is tracked for you. Flights are monitored in real time, so a delayed arrival moves the pickup time without a phone call. For a delayed train, call as early as possible, because waiting charges can apply when notice arrives less than two hours before the scheduled pickup.

  5. A first booking may need a card authorization. New clients can be asked to approve a card authorization on a Stripe payment page before a first reservation is confirmed. Card authorization takes three steps, and card numbers, card photos and ID copies are never requested by phone, email, text or WhatsApp.

Waiting charges vary by vehicle type and begin when the free window ends, counted from touchdown on airport arrivals and from the scheduled pickup time at every other pickup. If the passenger cannot be reached after several attempts, the reservation may be marked as a no-show and charged in full.

Meet and greet takes place inside the terminal. The chauffeur waits with a name sign, helps with luggage and walks you to the car. It is an add-on plus any applicable airport parking charges. Without it, the airport pickup is at the curb or the designated pickup area, with the chauffeur coordinating by phone or WhatsApp once you have your bags.

Infant, toddler and booster seats are available on request at no additional charge. Ask for them in the notes so the right seat is fitted before the car leaves.

Cancellation terms are confirmed in writing with the reservation. To change or cancel, call +1 (646) 775-0556 or message on WhatsApp with your reference, and give as much notice as you can.
