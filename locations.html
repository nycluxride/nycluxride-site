New York City coverage

Chauffeur service areas across New York City

Twelve areas across the five boroughs and both city airports, from Inwood to Tottenville and from the Financial District to Flushing.

Flat airport rates apply from every borough to LaGuardia, JFK, Newark Liberty and Teterboro. Every vehicle class books by the hour with a three hour minimum. See hourly rates by class.

Airports

Both New York City airports

John F. Kennedy International sits in Jamaica, Queens, and LaGuardia sits in East Elmhurst, closer to Manhattan than any other commercial field in the region. Newark Liberty and Teterboro are in New Jersey and carry the same flat rate as JFK from any city address.

  • JFK Airport

    Flight-timed pickups at every passenger terminal, on a flat rate.

  • LaGuardia Airport

    Terminals B and C off the Grand Central Parkway, on a flat rate.

Manhattan

Manhattan and four named neighborhoods

Manhattan coverage runs the whole island, from Inwood at the north tip to Battery Park City at the south. Four neighborhoods ask for different things, from evening pickups around Prince Street to weekday mornings on Wall Street. Hourly days across the island are set out under NYC chauffeur service.

  • Manhattan

    Inwood to Battery Park City, with every airport run starting at a river crossing.

  • SoHo

    Houston Street to Canal Street, with the Holland Tunnel on the corner.

  • Financial District

    Short one way lanes below Chambers Street, and West Street straight up to the Holland Tunnel for Newark.

  • Upper East Side

    Fifth Avenue to the East River, the closest Manhattan neighborhood to LaGuardia.

  • Upper West Side

    Central Park West to Riverside Drive, with a straight run up the Henry Hudson Parkway to Teterboro.

Queens

Queens and Long Island City

Queens holds both airports, which is why so much of the borough work is airport work. Long Island City sits on the East River waterfront directly opposite Midtown, joined to it by the Ed Koch Queensboro Bridge and the Queens-Midtown Tunnel, about five road miles from LaGuardia.

  • Queens

    Both airports, Citi Field and the US Open grounds at Flushing Meadows.

  • Long Island City

    One bridge and one tunnel from Midtown East, five road miles from LaGuardia.

Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island

The three remaining boroughs

Brooklyn reaches JFK by the Belt Parkway and Manhattan by four East River crossings. The Bronx is the only borough on the mainland. Staten Island connects to Brooklyn by the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge and to New Jersey by three more. Brooklyn vehicles and hourly work are set out under black car service in Brooklyn.

  • Brooklyn

    Greenpoint to Coney Island, with JFK the near field for most addresses.

  • The Bronx

    The mainland borough, with LaGuardia the closest airport to almost every address.

  • Staten Island

    Four bridges and a passenger-only ferry, with Newark the closest airport for most addresses.
