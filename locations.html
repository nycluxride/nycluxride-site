New York City coverage

Chauffeur service areas across New York City

We pick up at any address in the five boroughs and at JFK, LaGuardia, Newark and Teterboro. These pages are guides to the five boroughs, five neighborhoods and both city airports.

Airport rides have flat rates from all five boroughs to JFK, LaGuardia, Newark Liberty and Teterboro. You can book any class by the hour, with a 3-hour minimum. See hourly rates.

Airports

Both New York City airports

JFK is in Jamaica, Queens. LaGuardia is in East Elmhurst, the closest commercial airport to Manhattan. Newark Liberty and Teterboro are in New Jersey, and they have the same flat rate as JFK from any city address.

  • JFK Airport

    Flight-timed pickups at every passenger terminal, on a flat rate.

  • LaGuardia Airport

    Terminals B and C off the Grand Central Parkway, on a flat rate.

Manhattan

Manhattan and four neighborhoods

We cover all of Manhattan, from Inwood in the north to Battery Park City in the south. Four neighborhoods have their own pages: SoHo, the Financial District, the Upper East Side and the Upper West Side. For hourly days across the island, see NYC chauffeur service.

  • Manhattan

    Inwood to Battery Park City. Each airport trip starts with a river crossing.

  • SoHo

    Houston Street to Canal Street, with the Holland Tunnel on the corner.

  • Financial District

    Short one-way streets below Chambers Street, and West Street straight up to the Holland Tunnel for Newark.

  • Upper East Side

    Fifth Avenue to the East River, the closest Manhattan neighborhood to LaGuardia.

  • Upper West Side

    Central Park West to Riverside Drive, with a straight drive up the Henry Hudson Parkway to Teterboro.

Queens

Long Island City and the rest of the borough

Both city airports are in Queens. Long Island City is on the East River, right across from Midtown, and connected to it by the Ed Koch Queensboro Bridge and the Queens-Midtown Tunnel. LaGuardia is about 5 miles away by road.

  • Queens

    Both airports, Citi Field and the US Open grounds at Flushing Meadows.

  • Long Island City

    One bridge and one tunnel from Midtown East, about 5 miles from LaGuardia.

Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island

The three remaining boroughs

From Brooklyn, the Belt Parkway goes to JFK and four East River crossings go to Manhattan. The Bronx is the only borough on the mainland. Staten Island connects to Brooklyn by the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge and to New Jersey by three more bridges. For Brooklyn cars and hourly bookings, read black car service in Brooklyn.

  • Brooklyn

    Greenpoint to Coney Island, with JFK the closest airport for most addresses.

  • The Bronx

    The mainland borough, with LaGuardia the closest airport to almost every address.

  • Staten Island

    Four bridges and a passenger-only ferry, with Newark the closest airport for most addresses.
