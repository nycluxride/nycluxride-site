New York City coverage

Service areas

Twelve pages, grouped the way the city is actually laid out. Both airports first, then Manhattan and its named neighborhoods, then Queens, and then Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island.

Flat airport rates are $130 to LaGuardia and $165 to JFK, Newark Liberty and Teterboro. Hourly service starts at $85 an hour with a three hour minimum. All prices exclude taxes, tolls & gratuity. Final total confirmed at booking.

Airports

Both New York City airports

John F. Kennedy International sits in Jamaica, Queens, and LaGuardia sits in East Elmhurst, closer to Manhattan than any other commercial field in the region. Newark Liberty and Teterboro are in New Jersey and are quoted on the services and rates page rather than given their own area page.

    • On-time departures

Queens

Queens and Long Island City

Queens holds both airports, which is why so much of the borough work is airport work. Long Island City sits on the East River waterfront directly opposite Midtown, joined to it by the Ed Koch Queensboro Bridge and the Queens-Midtown Tunnel, so it has its own page.

  • Queens

    Private rides throughout Astoria, Flushing, Long Island City, and Jamaica.

    • LGA proximity
    • JFK area rides
    • Neighborhood coverage

  • Long Island City

    Corporate-ready black car coverage for LIC offices, hotels, and waterfront venues.

    • Office transport
    • Hotel transfers
    • Waterfront events

Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island

The three remaining boroughs

Each of these three has one page covering the whole borough. Brooklyn reaches JFK by the Belt Parkway and Manhattan by four East River crossings. The Bronx is the only borough on the mainland. Staten Island connects to Brooklyn by the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge and to New Jersey by three more.

  • Brooklyn

    Premium chauffeur service in Williamsburg, DUMBO, Park Slope, and beyond.

    • Venue transportation
    • Business travel
    • Airport drop-offs

  • The Bronx

    Reliable black car service for business, events, and airport trips in the Bronx.

    • Event drop-offs
    • Corporate trips
    • Inter-borough transfers

  • Staten Island

    Executive rides from Staten Island to NYC destinations and major airports.

    • Early airport departures
    • Round-trip bookings
    • Family travel
