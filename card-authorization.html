Payment

Card authorization

A card authorization is a signed approval to charge a named card for a named reservation. To secure the transaction and prevent fraud, new clients may be asked to complete one before a first reservation is confirmed.

How it works

  1. Book the ride. Reserve at customer.moovs.app, or call or message dispatch to arrange the trip.

  2. Dispatch sends a secure authorization request. The request arrives through the booking system and names the reservation it covers.

  3. You confirm and sign electronically. Review the details and approve the authorization with a secure electronic signature.

Your security

Card details are handled only through the PCI-compliant booking and payment system.

NYC LUX RIDE never asks for card numbers, photos of a card or copies of an ID by email, text message or web form. Any such request claiming to be NYC LUX RIDE is fraudulent. Do not respond, and report it to info@nycluxride.com.

Cancellation policy

Cancellation and refund terms for a reservation are stated at booking and confirmed in writing. Give as much notice as you can, and call +1 (646) 775-0556 if plans change close to pickup.

The terms of service cover billing and third-party payment providers in full.

What is never asked for

  • Card numbers, security codes or expiration dates sent by email, text message or a web form.
  • A photograph of a card, front or back.
  • A copy or photograph of a passport, driver's license or any other identity document.

Every one of those requests is a sign of fraud, whatever name or number it arrives from. Report it to info@nycluxride.com and call +1 (646) 775-0556 to check the status of a reservation.
