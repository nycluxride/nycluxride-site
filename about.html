New York City chauffeur and black car

About NYC LUX RIDE

A reserved car with a named vehicle class and a price agreed before the day of travel. Flat rate airport transfers, and hourly work with a three hour minimum.

What the service is

NYC LUX RIDE is a chauffeur and black car operator working out of New York City. Every trip is reserved ahead of time against a vehicle class, so the car and the rate are both settled before the pickup. On a flat rate airport transfer there is no meter and no distance surcharge.

Two kinds of work make up the schedule. Airport transfers are flat rated from any New York City address to JFK, Newark (EWR), Teterboro (TEB) and LaGuardia (LGA). Hourly and as directed work covers business meetings, events, shopping and multi-stop days, billed by vehicle class with a three hour minimum.

The fleet

Twelve vehicles run in four classes. Business SUV is the Cadillac Escalade ESV, GMC Denali, Chevrolet Suburban and Lincoln Navigator. First Class is the Mercedes-Benz S-Class and the BMW 7 Series. Sedan & Electric is the Cadillac Lyriq, Cadillac CT5 and CT6, Cadillac XT6 and Lincoln Continental. Luxury Sprinter is the Limo Sprinter and the Jet Sprinter, each seating up to twelve.

A Business SUV carries up to five passengers with three large suitcases and three carry-on bags. A First Class sedan carries up to three passengers with two large suitcases and one carry-on bag. The Sedan & Electric class has no published bag figure. Passenger and luggage figures are set out vehicle by vehicle.

Where the cars run

Pickups cover twelve areas: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx and Staten Island, along with Long Island City, SoHo, the Upper East Side, the Upper West Side and the Financial District, plus terminal pickups at JFK and LaGuardia. Airport work also runs to Newark (EWR) and Teterboro (TEB). Local routes and pickup detail for each area are set out under service areas.

What is included, and what is added

Arrivals are matched against live flight data, so a late landing moves the pickup time without a phone call. Meet and greet inside the terminal, with a personalized name sign and luggage help, is an add-on plus any airport parking charge. Without it, the pickup is at the curb or the designated pickup area, with the chauffeur coordinating by phone or WhatsApp once you have your bags.

Infant, toddler and booster seats are available on request at no extra charge. Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and charging ports are fitted in the executive fleet. Smoking is not permitted in any vehicle. Base rates are quoted separately from tolls and a standard gratuity, which are added to the final invoice.

Booking, accounts and payment

Booking at least 24 hours ahead is recommended so a preferred vehicle can be held, and same-day requests are accepted when a car is free. New clients may be asked to complete a credit card authorization form before a first reservation is confirmed. Corporate accounts are available with monthly billing, priority scheduling and dedicated support.

Reservations are completed on the booking system at customer.moovs.app, which is a separate site. Rates for every class are set out under car service rates, and nineteen common questions are answered in full.

Operating commitments

Four things the service holds itself to

  1. A flat rate on airport work. One flat fare to each airport from any New York City address. All prices exclude taxes, tolls & gratuity, and the final total is confirmed at booking.

  2. Flights monitored in real time. A delayed arrival moves the pickup time automatically rather than costing the passenger a phone call from the baggage hall.

  3. Waiting time published, not improvised. 45 minutes complimentary on domestic airport arrivals, 60 minutes on international arrivals, and a 15-minute grace period on every other pickup. After the grace period, waiting charges run from the originally scheduled pickup time and vary by vehicle type.

  4. A three hour minimum, stated before the day. Hourly and as directed work is billed by vehicle class, so the cost of a booked block is known before it starts.
