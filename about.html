About NYC LUX RIDE

NYC LUX RIDE is a chauffeur and black car service in New York City. We drive you to and from the airports at a flat rate, or by the hour.

What we do

You book each trip ahead for a vehicle class, and you know the car and the price before pickup. There's no meter and no distance surcharge on an airport ride.

We do two kinds of trips. Airport rides have a flat rate from any New York City address to JFK, LaGuardia, Newark Liberty (EWR) and Teterboro. Rides by the hour cover business meetings, events, shopping and multi-stop days. You can book any class by the hour, with a 3-hour minimum.

Our fleet

Our fleet has five classes. Business SUV is the GMC Denali, Chevrolet Suburban and Lincoln Navigator. Business Premium SUV is the Cadillac Escalade ESV. First Class is the Mercedes-Benz S-Class and the BMW 7 Series. Sedan & Electric is the Cadillac Lyriq, Cadillac CT5 or CT6, Cadillac XT6 and Lincoln Continental. Luxury Sprinter is the Limo Sprinter and the Jet Sprinter, each for up to 12 passengers.

A Business SUV or Business Premium SUV takes up to 5 passengers with 3 large and 3 small bags. A First Class sedan takes up to 3 passengers with 2 large and 1 small bag. We don't publish a bag figure for Sedan & Electric. See the fleet for the seats and bags of each vehicle.

Where we pick up

We pick up anywhere in the five boroughs and at JFK, LaGuardia, Newark and Teterboro. Our guides cover 12 areas: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, Long Island City, SoHo, the Upper East Side, the Upper West Side, the Financial District, JFK and LaGuardia. The service areas page has routes and pickup details for each one.

What the price covers

Meet and greet is an add-on, and you also pay any airport parking. With meet and greet, your chauffeur waits inside the terminal with a name sign and helps with your bags. Without it, your chauffeur meets you at the curb or the car service pickup area. Once you have your bags, they call or message you with the exact spot.

Infant, toddler and booster seats are free. Tell us which one you need. The executive fleet has free Wi-Fi and charging ports. There's no smoking in any of our vehicles. Airport transfers are one total price with tolls, airport fees and taxes included, and the gratuity for your chauffeur is optional. On hourly bookings, tolls and parking are charged after the ride to the card saved at booking, each with an itemized receipt.

How to book

Book online at least 24 hours ahead. We take same-day rides when a car is free. Before your first ride, we may ask for a card authorization. Corporate accounts get monthly billing and priority scheduling.

Book online, by phone or on WhatsApp. Payments go through Stripe. See car service rates for each class, and read the FAQ.

Our policies

Rates and pickups

  1. Flat airport rates. We charge one fare by vehicle class to each of the four airports, from any New York City address. Airport transfer prices include tolls, airport fees and taxes. Hourly rates are before NYC sales tax, the Black Car Fund, tolls and parking, and the gratuity is optional.

  2. Flight tracking. We track your flight and move the pickup if it lands early or late. You don't have to call us from baggage claim.

  3. Free waiting. You get 45 minutes of free waiting after a domestic landing, 60 minutes after an international landing, and 15 minutes at any other pickup. After that, we charge for waiting at a rate that depends on the vehicle. For flights, the free waiting starts at touchdown. For other pickups, it starts at the pickup time.

  4. A 3-hour minimum. Each class has its own hourly rate, and you know the cost of your booking before it starts.
