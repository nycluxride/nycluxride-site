About NYC LUX RIDE

A reserved car with a named vehicle class and a price agreed before the day of travel. Flat rate airport transfers, and hourly work with a three hour minimum.

What the service is

NYC LUX RIDE is a chauffeur and black car operator working out of New York City. Every trip is reserved ahead of time against a vehicle class, so the car and the rate are both settled before the pickup. On a flat rate airport transfer there is no meter and no distance surcharge.

Two kinds of work make up the schedule. Airport transfers are flat rated from New York City at $165 to JFK, Newark (EWR) and Teterboro (TEB), and $130 to LaGuardia (LGA). Hourly and as directed work covers business meetings, events, shopping and multi-stop days, charged from $85 to $175 an hour by vehicle class, with a three hour minimum.

The fleet, as listed

Twelve vehicles are published across four classes. Business SUV is the Cadillac Escalade ESV, GMC Denali, Chevrolet Suburban and Lincoln Navigator. First Class is the Mercedes-Benz S-Class and the BMW 7 Series. Sedan & Electric is the Cadillac Lyriq, Cadillac CT5 and CT6, Cadillac XT6 and Lincoln Continental. Luxury Sprinter is the Limo Sprinter and the Jet Sprinter, each seating up to twelve.

An SUV carries up to five passengers with three large suitcases and three carry-on bags. A sedan carries up to three passengers with two large suitcases and two carry-on bags. Passenger and luggage figures for each vehicle are on the fleet page.

Where the cars run

Coverage is published for twelve areas: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx and Staten Island, along with Long Island City, SoHo, the Upper East Side, the Upper West Side and the Financial District, plus terminal pickups at JFK and LaGuardia. Airport work also runs to Newark (EWR) and Teterboro (TEB). Every area has its own page under service areas.

What is included, and what is added

Arrivals are matched against live flight data, so a late landing moves the pickup time without a phone call. Meet and greet inside the terminal, with a personalized name sign and luggage help, is an additional $25 plus any airport parking charge. At JFK, pickups happen inside the terminal near baggage claim.

Infant, toddler and booster seats are available on request at no extra charge. Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and charging ports are fitted in the executive fleet. Smoking is not permitted in any vehicle. Base rates are quoted separately from tolls and a standard 20% gratuity, which are added to the final invoice.

Booking, accounts and payment

Booking at least 24 hours ahead is recommended so a preferred vehicle can be held, and same-day requests are accepted when a car is free. New clients may be asked to complete a credit card authorization form before a first reservation is confirmed. Corporate accounts are available with monthly billing, priority scheduling and dedicated support.

Reservations are completed on the booking system at customer.moovs.app, which is a separate site. Rates for every class are on the services page, and the full question set is on the FAQ page.

Operating commitments

Four things the service holds itself to

  1. A flat rate on airport work. $165 to JFK, Newark (EWR) and Teterboro (TEB). $130 to LaGuardia (LGA). All prices exclude taxes, tolls & gratuity, and the final total is confirmed at booking.

  2. Flights monitored in real time. A delayed arrival moves the pickup time automatically rather than costing the passenger a phone call from the baggage hall.

  3. Waiting time published, not improvised. 45 minutes complimentary on domestic airport arrivals, 60 minutes on international arrivals, and a 15-minute grace period on every other pickup. After the grace period, waiting charges run from the originally scheduled pickup time and vary by vehicle type.

  4. A three hour minimum, stated before the day. Hourly and as directed work runs from $85 to $175 an hour by vehicle class, so the cost of a booked block is known before it starts.
