Teterboro Airport FBOs and the drive into Manhattan

November 19, 2026, 5 min read

Teterboro is the private jet airport closest to Midtown. This guide shows where each FBO is and which crossing gets you into Manhattan.

In short

  • Teterboro has five FBOs and no airline terminal. Your car goes to the FBO your flight uses.
  • For Midtown and Lower Manhattan, the car takes the Lincoln or Holland Tunnel. Uptown trips cross the George Washington Bridge.
  • With light traffic, Midtown is close to 25 minutes from the FBOs.
  • A ride between Teterboro and any New York City address is one flat price by class.

Five FBOs and no airline terminal

Teterboro Airport is in Bergen County, New Jersey, about 12 miles north-northwest of Midtown. It spreads over the boroughs of Teterboro, Moonachie and Hasbrouck Heights. The Port Authority of New York and New Jersey owns it.

Jets heavier than 100,000 pounds can't take off here. There are no scheduled airline flights. The planes on the ramp are private and corporate jets, plus a few charter operators.

FBO is short for fixed base operator. It is the private aviation building where your plane parks, with its own lobby, its own parking lot and its own street address. Teterboro has five of them. Your chauffeur can meet you in the lobby or at the aircraft, subject to airport access and rules.

  • Atlantic Aviation, 233 Industrial Avenue
  • Signature Aviation West, 485 Industrial Avenue
  • Jet Aviation, 112 Charles A. Lindbergh Drive
  • Signature Aviation South, 101 Charles A. Lindbergh Drive
  • Signature Aviation East, 200 Fred Wehran Drive

Atlantic and Signature West are both on Industrial Avenue, on the west side of the airport. Signature East is on Fred Wehran Drive, on the east side.

Which FBO your flight uses

Three of the five buildings carry the Signature name. Signature East and Signature West are on opposite sides of the airport. A booking for the wrong one sends the car to the wrong lobby.

Your charter operator, the flight department or the crew can tell you where the plane will park. Ask for the full name. For a Signature building, that means East, South or West as well.

Private flights change. A departure slips by a few hours, or the plane moves to another FBO. When that happens, email info@nycluxride.com or message us on WhatsApp with your reference and the new details. We update the booking and the chauffeur goes to the new address.

Lincoln Tunnel or George Washington Bridge

Every trip from Teterboro into Manhattan crosses the Hudson. The FBOs are close to US Route 46 and Route 17, and from there the car heads for one of three crossings. The right one depends on where in the city you are going.

For Midtown, Hudson Yards and Times Square, the car takes Route 3 east to Route 495 and the Lincoln Tunnel. It comes out on the West Side of Midtown, a short drive from Times Square.

For the Upper West Side, the Upper East Side and Harlem, the George Washington Bridge is the better way in. The car crosses the bridge and heads down the Henry Hudson Parkway. For the East Side, it goes across Central Park on the 79th Street Transverse.

For Lower Manhattan and Brooklyn, the car follows Route 3 to US 1 and 9, then Route 139 to the Holland Tunnel. From the Manhattan end of the tunnel, Wall Street is a short drive south. Downtown Brooklyn is over the Manhattan Bridge.

For Queens and the two city airports, the car goes over the George Washington Bridge and along the Cross Bronx Expressway. LaGuardia is about 17 miles from Teterboro, and JFK about 28. Traffic on the day can change the best crossing for any of these trips.

How long the drive takes

These distances and times start at the FBOs and assume light traffic. They come from driving routes on OpenStreetMap road data.

  • Midtown is about 13 miles by the Lincoln Tunnel, close to 25 minutes.
  • The Upper West Side is about 14 miles over the bridge, also close to 25 minutes.
  • The Upper East Side is about 15 miles, close to 30 minutes.
  • The Financial District is about 17 miles by the Holland Tunnel, about 30 minutes.
  • Downtown Brooklyn is about 18 miles, about 30 minutes.
  • LaGuardia is about 17 miles, about 35 minutes.
  • JFK is about 28 miles, about 45 minutes.

At rush hour the same trips take longer. The lines at the tunnels and the bridge grow on weekday mornings and evenings, and holiday weekends add more. We don't give rush hour times here because they change from one day to the next.

For a departure, ask your operator how early the crew wants you at the FBO. Give us that time when you book, and we set the pickup with room to spare.

The price doesn't change with traffic. One total by class from any New York City address, with tolls, airport fees and the service fee included. The Teterboro rates are the same as JFK in a car or SUV.

Booking a car to your FBO

On our booking page, type Teterboro in the pickup or the drop-off. The list shows all five FBOs by name. Choose yours, and the car goes to that street address. Our Teterboro Airport car service page lists the six main routes into the city.

Add the date and the time you land, or the time you need to leave. If someone else is flying, choose Someone else is riding and add their full name and mobile number. The chauffeur contacts the passenger on the day, and the confirmation and receipt go to your email.

Book online at least 90 minutes before pickup. For a pickup within the next 90 minutes, email or WhatsApp us.

Flying from another private airport around New York, like Westchester, Morristown or Republic, books the same way, and you see the price when you book. Our private jet car service page has the FBOs at all six. A company that books cars every month can open a corporate account with monthly billing.

Families and dogs on a private flight

Families flying private can add child, infant and booster seats when they book. Count each child as a passenger. Child, infant and booster seats are $20 per seat, charged at booking. We fit them before the car leaves for the airport.

Dogs can come too. Choose a small or large dog under Pets, and tell us about your dog in the notes. Our pet friendly car service page shows which car gives a big dog the most room. Service animals ride free.

For bags, a First Class sedan takes up to 3 passengers with 2 large bags and 1 small bag. Both SUV classes take up to 6 passengers with 3 large and 3 small bags. For a full crew or a large party, the Luxury Sprinter seats up to 14 with room for 10 large and 2 small bags. See the fleet.

Trips beyond Manhattan

Some flights land at Teterboro for a weekend outside the city. We drive from the FBOs to New Jersey towns, Westchester and Connecticut, and out to the Hamptons. Greenwich is about 33 miles away, close to 50 minutes with light traffic. Southampton, in the Hamptons, is about 98 miles.

These trips leave the flat rate. You see the price when you book, and you can pay online.

A connection to an airline flight is just as easy. Book the FBO as the pickup and JFK or LaGuardia as the drop-off. A trip between two airports is priced when you book.
