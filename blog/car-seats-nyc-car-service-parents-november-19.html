What parents ask about car seats in NYC car services

November 19, 2026, 5 min read

Parents ask us the same few questions before a ride with children. Here are the answers, from booking a seat to a long drive with kids.

In short

  • Child, infant and booster seats are $20 per seat, charged at booking.
  • One charge per seat covers the ride home too.
  • Count every child as a passenger, babies included.
  • On airport pickups, the seats are fitted before the car leaves for JFK, LaGuardia, Newark or Teterboro.

Do car services have car seats

Some do and some don't. Ask before you book, and ask what kind of seat it is. Ask how many seats the car can bring, too. A seat for a newborn is a different thing from a booster for a seven-year-old.

Ride apps have their own options. At least one app in New York offers car seat rides by reservation, with one seat per car, for a child from 5 to 65 pounds. That covers one child. With two children, or a baby and an older brother or sister, you need more than one seat in the car.

We carry three kinds of seat. The child seat faces forward or rear, the infant seat faces rear and the booster is for older children. You choose them for each child when you book, as many as you have children, and we fit them before the car leaves. Our car service with car seats in NYC page has the details in one place.

Which seat for which age

The American Academy of Pediatrics gives parents a clear order. Babies and young children face the rear for as long as their seat allows, up to its top weight or height. After that comes a forward-facing seat with a harness. The AAP suggests keeping that seat at least to age 4.

Then a booster, until the adult seat belt fits. The AAP says that point comes when a child is about 4 feet 9 inches tall and between 8 and 12 years old. It also says all children under 13 should ride in the back seat.

Pick the seat by your child's size today, and add their age, weight and height in the notes. Every child grows at their own pace, and the right seat follows the child.

Booking a seat

On the booking page, open Child seats and pick how many child, infant and booster seats you need. The note under the seat choices reminds you to add each child's age, weight and height. Each seat is charged once, even on a round trip. Our seats are the Nuna RAVA, the Safety 1st Grow and Go and a backless booster.

Count every child as a passenger when you choose the car. The booking won't let you add more seats than passengers. A First Class sedan takes up to 3 passengers, and both SUV classes take up to 6. For a big family or two families together, the Luxury Sprinter seats up to 14.

You can also book by phone. Call +1 (646) 775-0556, email info@nycluxride.com or message us on WhatsApp, and tell us the seats and each child's age, weight and height. Mention a stroller in the notes too.

Airport pickups with children

For a flight home, give us your flight number when you book. We track your flight and move the pickup if it lands early or late. You get 45 minutes of free waiting after a domestic landing and 60 minutes after an international landing.

The seats are already in the car when it reaches the airport. Your chauffeur waits nearby while you collect your bags, then comes to the curb or the car service pickup area. For now, car service pickups at Terminals 5 and 7 use a lot at the Howard Beach AirTrain station, a free AirTrain ride from the terminal.

With small children and a cart of suitcases, meet and greet helps. Your chauffeur waits inside the terminal with a name sign, helps with the bags and walks you to the car. It costs extra, plus any airport parking. See the add-ons.

Rides between New York City and JFK, LaGuardia, Newark or Teterboro have one flat price by class, and the seats are added to it when you book. For terminal details, see our JFK car service, LaGuardia, Newark Airport and Teterboro pages.

Seats on long trips

The same seats come on long drives. Philadelphia is close to 2 hours from Midtown with light traffic. Boston takes about 4 hours 30 minutes and Washington close to 4 hours 50 minutes. That is a long time to spend in a car seat.

Add up to 2 stops when you book, for lunch or a break. Pack snacks, a change of clothes and something to do. Downloaded shows and books help on the highway.

Our long distance car service page lists the main trips with their drive times. For a family weekend in Pennsylvania, our guide to New York to Philadelphia with kids compares the drive with the train.

Kids and a dog can share one car. Add the seats and the dog on the same booking. Our pet friendly car service page explains which car gives you room for both.

Visits outside the city

Seats come on trips out of town too. A family visiting grandparents on Long Island or in Westchester books the same way, with the seats chosen on the booking page and each child's age, weight and height in the notes.

Families in Hoboken and Jersey City can ride to JFK or LaGuardia with the seats already fitted. Our Hoboken page has the roads and the drive times to each airport.

Trips that leave the city book the same way. Priced by distance and time. You see the total before you pay.

For a weekend away, add the ride home when you book. The seats stay on the booking for both rides, and you pay for them once.

Hourly days with children

For a day of stops in the city, book by the hour. A doctor's visit, a school tour and lunch can all go on one booking. You can book a sedan or an SUV by the hour, with a 3-hour minimum. A Sprinter by the hour has a 4-hour minimum.

The seats stay fitted in the car between stops, and the same chauffeur stays with you all day. Strollers and shopping bags stay in the back while you are out. Put the stops and the times in the notes when you book. See our hourly chauffeur service for the full terms.
