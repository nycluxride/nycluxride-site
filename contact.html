Service area

There is no walk-in office and no published street address, so there is no map on this page. Pickups are arranged across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx and Staten Island, and at JFK, LaGuardia, Newark (EWR) and Teterboro (TEB). Each area has its own page under service areas.

Booking off site

Reservations are completed on the booking system at customer.moovs.app, which is a separate site run for NYC LUX RIDE. It asks for the pickup date and time, the pickup and drop-off points, the number of passengers and bags, a vehicle class, and a name and contact number. Rates for every class are listed on the services page first.
