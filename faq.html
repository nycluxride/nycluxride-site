Reservations

When is the best time to schedule my ride?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to ensure availability and preferred vehicle selection. Same-day requests are accepted when possible. For reservations, call +1 (646) 775-0556.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reservations canceled more than 24 hours before pickup are fully refundable. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a service fee.

Do you offer hourly chauffeur service?

Yes. Our hourly service is ideal for business meetings, events, shopping trips, and multi-stop travel throughout New York City and surrounding areas at $100 per hour with a 3-hour minimum.

Airport Transportation

What does your Meet & Greet service include?

Your professional chauffeur will welcome you inside the terminal with a personalized name sign, assist with your luggage, and escort you directly to your vehicle.

Is there an extra charge for airport Meet & Greet service?

Yes. NYC LUX RIDE applies an additional $25 fee for Meet & Greet service, plus any applicable airport parking charges.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor all flights in real time. If your flight is delayed, your chauffeur will automatically adjust the pickup time accordingly.

Where will I meet my chauffeur at JFK?

At John F. Kennedy International Airport, pickups are conducted inside the terminal near baggage claim for convenience and discretion.

Train Transportation

What happens if my train is delayed?

If your train is delayed, please contact NYC LUX RIDE at +1 (646) 775-0556 as soon as possible so we can adjust your pickup based on availability.

Are there extra charges if my train is delayed?

If we are not notified at least two hours before your scheduled pickup time, waiting time charges may apply.

Waiting Time Policy

Do you offer free waiting time for pickups?

Yes. We provide 45 minutes complimentary for domestic airport arrivals, 60 minutes complimentary for international arrivals, and a 15-minute grace period for all other pickups.

Will I be charged if I exceed the waiting time?

Yes. After the grace period ends, waiting time charges begin from the original scheduled pickup time and vary by vehicle type. If we cannot reach you after multiple attempts, the reservation may be marked as a no-show and charged in full.

Fleet & Amenities

How many passengers and bags can your SUVs accommodate?

Luxury SUV: up to 5 passengers with 3 large suitcases plus 3 small carry-on bags. Luxury Sedan: up to 3 passengers with 2 large suitcases plus 2 small carry-on bags.

Are child safety seats available?

Yes. Infant, toddler, and booster seats are available upon request at no additional charge.

Do your vehicles include WiFi?

Yes. Complimentary high-speed WiFi and charging ports are available in our executive fleet.

Is smoking allowed in your vehicles?

No. Smoking is strictly prohibited in all NYC LUX RIDE vehicles to maintain a clean and comfortable environment.

Pricing & Payment

Are tolls and gratuity included in the rate?

Base rates are quoted separately. Applicable tolls and a standard 20% gratuity are added to the final invoice.

What are your business luxury SUV base airport rates?

Business luxury SUV airport rates are $165 for JFK, EWR, and Teterboro, and $130 for LGA.

Why is a credit card authorization form required?

To ensure secure transactions and prevent fraud, new clients may be required to complete a credit card authorization form before confirming their first reservation.

Do you offer corporate accounts?

Yes. We provide corporate accounts with monthly billing, priority scheduling, and dedicated support.
