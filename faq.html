Reservations

When is the best time to schedule my ride?

Book at least 24 hours in advance to hold the vehicle class you want. Same-day requests are accepted when a car is free. For reservations, call +1 (646) 775-0556.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and refund terms for a reservation are stated at booking and confirmed in writing. Give as much notice as you can, and call +1 (646) 775-0556 if plans change close to pickup.

Do you offer hourly chauffeur service?

Yes. Hourly and as directed work is billed by vehicle class with a three hour minimum, and suits business meetings, events, shopping and multi-stop days across New York City. See hourly rates by class.

Airport transportation

What does meet and greet include?

The chauffeur waits inside the terminal with a personalized name sign, helps with luggage and walks you to the car. Without meet and greet, the pickup is at the curb or the designated pickup area, arranged by phone or WhatsApp once you have your bags.

Is there an extra charge for meet and greet?

Yes. Meet and greet is an add-on to the fare, plus any applicable airport parking charge.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Flights are tracked in real time, so a delay moves the pickup time rather than the price. Complimentary waiting is counted from touchdown, 45 minutes on domestic arrivals and 60 on international arrivals.

Where will I meet my chauffeur at JFK?

At the curb or the designated pickup area for your terminal. The chauffeur tracks the flight and confirms the exact spot by phone or WhatsApp once you have your bags. At Terminals 5 and 7, car service pickups use a lot at the Howard Beach AirTrain station, a free AirTrain ride from the terminal, as of September 2026. With meet and greet, the chauffeur waits inside near baggage claim with a name sign, as an add-on plus any airport parking charge.

Train transportation

What happens if my train is delayed?

If a train is delayed, call NYC LUX RIDE at +1 (646) 775-0556 as soon as possible so the pickup can be moved, subject to availability.

Are there extra charges if my train is delayed?

Waiting charges can apply if dispatch is not told of the delay at least two hours before the scheduled pickup time.

Waiting time policy

Do you offer free waiting time for pickups?

Yes. Domestic airport arrivals include 45 minutes of complimentary waiting, international arrivals 60 minutes, and every other pickup a 15 minute grace period.

Will I be charged if I exceed the waiting time?

Yes. After the grace period, waiting charges run from the originally scheduled pickup time and vary by vehicle type. If the passenger cannot be reached after several attempts, the reservation may be marked as a no-show and charged in full.

Fleet & amenities

How many passengers and bags can your SUVs accommodate?

A Business SUV takes up to 5 passengers with 3 large suitcases and 3 carry-on bags. A First Class sedan takes up to 3 passengers with 2 large suitcases and 1 carry-on bag. A Luxury Sprinter seats 12 with 10 large and 2 small bags. The Sedan & Electric class has no published bag figure, so confirm cases when booking.

Are child safety seats available?

Yes. Infant, toddler and booster seats are available on request at no extra charge. Name the seat in the booking notes so the right one is fitted before the car leaves.

Do your vehicles include Wi-Fi?

Yes. Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and charging ports are fitted in the executive fleet.

Is smoking allowed in your vehicles?

No. Smoking is not permitted in any NYC LUX RIDE vehicle.

Pricing & payment

Are tolls and gratuity included in the rate?

No. Base rates are quoted separately. Taxes, applicable tolls and a standard gratuity are added to the final invoice, and the final total is confirmed at booking.

What are the airport rates in a Business SUV?

Airport transfers are one flat rate from any New York City address, and hourly work is billed by vehicle class with a three hour minimum. Every rate is on the rates page.

Why is a credit card authorization form required?

To secure the transaction and prevent fraud, new clients may be asked to complete a credit card authorization form before a first reservation is confirmed. It is signed electronically through the booking system, and card numbers are never requested by email, text or web form.

Do you offer corporate accounts?

Yes. Corporate accounts are available with monthly billing, priority scheduling and dedicated support, so a month of rides lands on one invoice. A first reservation may need one card authorization form.
