Black car service in Brooklyn, from Williamsburg to Bay Ridge

Flat airport rates to JFK, LaGuardia, Newark and Teterboro, hourly borough work by vehicle class, and a pickup window set from your block rather than from an average.

Brooklyn is not one pickup problem

A Williamsburg pickup on a Saturday night is a parking problem. A Park Slope pickup at seven in the morning is a one way street problem. A Bay Ridge pickup for a six o'clock flight is a distance problem, because the Belt Parkway runs the length of the shoreline between that address and JFK.

So the pickup window is built from the address, not from a borough average. Dispatch fixes it at booking, which is how the chauffeur is on the block before the time on the reservation rather than driving toward it.

Twelve neighborhoods account for most of the borough work. Each note below is the part of that neighborhood that changes the pickup.

Where a Brooklyn pickup goes wrong

Almost every late pickup in the borough has a physical cause on the block rather than a dispatch cause. A bus lane the car cannot legally sit in. A bike lane between the curb and the vehicle. A delivery truck double parked outside the only usable door. Alternate side parking that has emptied one side of a street and packed the other.

Two extra lines of information fix most of that. The cross street, and which side of the building you will walk out of. On a cobblestone block in DUMBO, or a one way stretch in Cobble Hill, those two lines are worth more than a five minute head start.

For a walk-up with a buzzer rather than a doorman, a phone number somebody will actually pick up is the third useful line. The chauffeur calls instead of standing silently at the curb wondering which of four identical brownstones is the right one.

Winter adds one more. Snow that has been pushed into the parking lane turns a normal curbside pickup into a walk of ten yards over an ice ridge, and the meeting point moves to the corner. Dispatch will say so when it books rather than leaving the chauffeur to negotiate it in the dark.

Routes out of the borough

JFK from most of Brooklyn is the Belt Parkway. It is the shortest line on the map and the least predictable one in practice, so the pickup window is built around the approach to the airport rather than around a clear run.

LaGuardia is the Brooklyn Queens Expressway north and then the Grand Central Parkway. The BQE through Downtown Brooklyn and Williamsburg is the slow section, so a LaGuardia departure out of South Brooklyn is often a longer trip than a JFK departure even though its flat rate is lower. Airport routes from Brooklyn and Queens compares the BQE, Belt Parkway and Van Wyck runs.

Into Manhattan there are four crossings and they are not interchangeable. The Brooklyn Bridge lands at the Civic Center. The Manhattan Bridge lands at Canal Street and Chinatown. The Williamsburg Bridge lands on Delancey Street on the Lower East Side. The Hugh L. Carey Tunnel, still called the Brooklyn Battery by most riders, lands in the Financial District and is tolled. The chauffeur picks by destination and hour, not by habit.

Newark and Teterboro both mean crossing Manhattan or Staten Island first, which is why they carry the same flat rate as JFK despite being further out.

  • LaGuardiaFlat rate, the BQE then the Grand Central

Cruise terminal

The Brooklyn Cruise Terminal at Red Hook

Brooklyn embarkation happens at Pier 12 in Red Hook, reached off Bowne Street. Red Hook has no subway station, which is most of the reason cruise day in this part of the borough is a black car day.

A cruise morning moves a lot of luggage against a fixed deadline, so bag capacity picks the vehicle before seat count does. A Business SUV takes 3 large plus 3 small bags for up to 5 passengers. A Luxury Sprinter takes 10 large plus 2 small for up to 12. Either can be booked hourly when the day has stops before the pier.

  • TerminalPier 12, Red Hook, off Bowne Street
  • CapacityUp to 10 large bags in a Luxury Sprinter
  • TimingEarly pickup windows on embarkation day

Questions about Brooklyn pickups

All nineteen questions

How do I book black car service in Brooklyn?

Book online on the booking page or call +1 (646) 775-0556 with the Brooklyn pickup address, the destination, the date and time and the passenger count. Dispatch replies in writing with the vehicle class, the fare and a pickup window set from that address rather than from a borough average.

How much does black car service in Brooklyn cost?

Airport transfers are flat rated from any New York City address. Hourly work is billed by vehicle class with a three hour minimum, which is what a borough day with several stops is usually booked as. See all rates.

Which bridge or tunnel does a trip into Manhattan use?

It depends on the destination and the hour. The Brooklyn Bridge lands at the Civic Center, the Manhattan Bridge at Canal Street, the Williamsburg Bridge at Delancey Street, and the Hugh L. Carey Tunnel in the Financial District, which is tolled.

Which Brooklyn neighborhoods do you pick up in?

Williamsburg, Greenpoint, DUMBO, Brooklyn Heights, Cobble Hill, Downtown Brooklyn, Park Slope, Prospect Heights, Bedford-Stuyvesant, Bushwick, Sunset Park and Bay Ridge, with cross-borough runs into Manhattan, Queens and the Bronx.

Do you serve the Brooklyn Cruise Terminal?

Yes. The Brooklyn Cruise Terminal is at Pier 12 in Red Hook, reached off Bowne Street. Embarkation day rewards an early pickup window and a vehicle chosen for bag capacity rather than seat count, which is 3 large plus 3 small in a Business SUV and 10 large plus 2 small in a Luxury Sprinter.
