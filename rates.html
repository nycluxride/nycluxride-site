Airport transfers

Each transfer is a one-way, flat-rate ride to or from any address in the five boroughs. We track your flight and move the pickup if it lands early or late. The price stays the same.

Pickup details for JFK, LaGuardia and Newark are on their airport pages.

Airport rides to or from a place outside New York City are priced by distance and time, and you see the total before you pay. Tolls are added when you book, and parking is charged after the ride.

One-way trips by distance and time

Other one-way trips are priced by distance and time. Under 50 miles, you pay a base rate plus a rate per mile and per minute. From 75 miles, the long-distance rates apply, and in between the price blends the two.

Each stop adds $20, or more if it takes the car out of the way. Tolls are added to your price when you book, and trips into New Jersey include the chauffeur's return crossing. Parking is charged after the ride. For very long trips, we'll send you a price.

Add-ons and terms

Meet and greet
Inside the terminal with a name sign, $25 charged at booking, plus any airport parking charged after the ride
Gratuity
Optional. Choose 10%, 15%, 20% or 25% of the fare, or your own amount, before you pay
Tolls and airport fees
Included on airport rides to or from New York City. On distance trips, tolls are added at booking. On hourly trips, they are charged to your card after the ride
Service fee
12% of the fare, added at booking on hourly and distance trips. Airport prices already include it
Free waiting at airports
45 minutes after a domestic landing and 60 after an international landing, counted from touchdown
Other pickups
15 minutes from the pickup time
Extra waiting
$1.20 to $4.00 a minute after the free waiting, by vehicle class
Extra stops
$20 per stop, charged at booking, or more if the stop takes the car out of the way. A stop added during an airport ride is charged after the ride
Child seats
Child, infant and booster seats, $20 per seat, charged once at booking, even on a round trip
Pets
$20 per small dog and $45 per large dog, charged once at booking, even on a round trip. Service animals ride free
Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup

All prices in USD. Hourly and distance prices exclude the service fee, tolls and parking.

Policies

Waiting

You get 45 minutes of free waiting after a domestic landing, 60 minutes after an international landing, and 15 minutes at any other pickup. After the free waiting, we charge by the minute at the rate for your vehicle.

You pay the total at checkout when you book. After the ride, we only charge the same card for extra waiting, stops added during the ride, parking, and tolls not in your price. Each charge comes with an itemized receipt.

Cancellation

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup.
  • Under 24 hours, the charge is 25% of the price.
  • Under 12 hours, it's 50%.
  • Under 2 hours, it's the full price.

To change or cancel, email info@nycluxride.com or message us on WhatsApp with your reference.

No-shows

If you're not at the pickup when your free waiting ends and haven't asked us to change or cancel, your chauffeur tries to reach you. If we still can't reach you, it's a no-show. The full price applies, with no waiting charge on top.

Changes and special bookings

Changes depend on availability and can change the price. Events, holidays, groups and custom trips can have a different cancellation deadline. If yours does, we tell you by email or WhatsApp before you pay.

Questions

Questions about rates

How much does a car service to the airport cost?

From $145 to LaGuardia and $170 to JFK, Newark or Teterboro in an Executive Sedan, from any New York City address. Tolls, airport fees and the service fee are included, and the price stays the same in any traffic. Rides from outside the city are priced by distance and time.

How much is a car from Manhattan to JFK?

$185 in a Business SUV from anywhere in New York City, with tolls, airport fees and the service fee included. Executive Sedan is $170, Electric Class $180, Business Premium SUV $220, First Class $310 and Luxury Sprinter from $450. A gratuity is optional.

How much is a car to LaGuardia?

$165 in a Business SUV from anywhere in New York City, with tolls, airport fees and the service fee included. Executive Sedan is $145, Electric Class $155, Business Premium SUV $190, First Class $250 and Luxury Sprinter from $450.

How much is a car service by the hour in NYC?

From $85 per hour in an Executive Sedan and from $95 in a Business SUV, with a 3-hour minimum, or 4 hours in a Sprinter. A 12% service fee is added at booking, and tolls and parking are charged after the ride.

What is the shortest hourly booking?

Three hours, or four in a Sprinter. For an Executive Sedan that's $255 plus the service fee, with tolls and parking after the ride.

When is my fare confirmed?

We confirm your car, class and fare in writing before the day of your ride.
