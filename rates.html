Rates

Car service rates in New York City

Airport transfers are one total price by vehicle class from any New York City address, with tolls, airport fees and taxes included. Hourly work is billed by vehicle class with a three hour minimum. There is no meter and no surge multiplier, so the price does not move with demand.

Airport transfers

Each transfer is a one-way, flat-rate ride to or from any address in the five boroughs. We track your flight and move the pickup if it lands early or late. The price stays the same.

All prices in USD. Airport totals include tolls, airport fees and taxes. Hourly rates are before tax, tolls and parking. A gratuity is optional. The fare for your trip comes back in writing before the day.

Cancellation and charges after the ride

Cancellation terms are confirmed in writing with the reservation. To change or cancel, call +1 (646) 775-0556 or message on WhatsApp with your reference, and give as much notice as you can.

At booking you pay the total shown at checkout: on airport transfers the fare, NYC sales tax, the Black Car Fund and tolls and airport fees, on hourly bookings the hourly fare with NYC sales tax and the Black Car Fund, plus meet and greet and a gratuity if you choose them. The card used at booking is saved for the charges known only after the ride: on airport transfers waiting beyond the free window, extra stops and parking, and on hourly bookings tolls and parking. Each is charged with an itemized receipt.

To ask about a charge, call +1 (646) 775-0556 or message us on WhatsApp with your reference.

Questions

Questions about rates

How much is a car from Manhattan to JFK?

$185 in a Business SUV, from any New York City address. That total includes the $155 fare, NYC sales tax, the Black Car Fund, and tolls and airport fees. Sedan & Electric is $170, Business Premium SUV $220, First Class $310 and Luxury Sprinter $400. A gratuity for your chauffeur is optional.

How much is a car to LaGuardia?

$165 in a Business SUV, from any New York City address. Sedan & Electric is $145, Business Premium SUV $190, First Class $250 and Luxury Sprinter $350. Each class costs less to LaGuardia than to JFK, Newark or Teterboro.

What is the shortest hourly booking?

Three hours. In the Sedan & Electric class that is $255 before NYC sales tax and the Black Car Fund, with tolls and parking charged after the ride.

When is my fare confirmed?

The vehicle, the class and the fare come back in writing before the day.
