Airport transfers

JFK, LaGuardia, Newark and Teterboro

The flat rate holds whatever the traffic does. Nothing on the invoice moves because of demand.

Flights are monitored in real time and the pickup time moves with the schedule when a flight is delayed. At John F. Kennedy International Airport the chauffeur meets you at the curb or the designated pickup area and confirms the spot by phone or WhatsApp once you have your bags. Meet and greet inside the terminal, with a name sign, is an add-on plus any airport parking charge.

  • Flight trackingPickup times adjust when the arrival slips
  • Waiting time45 minutes domestic, 60 minutes international
  • Luggage3 large plus 3 small bags in Business SUV

Hourly and as directed

Hourly chauffeur service in Manhattan

Hourly service suits a day with several stops. Midtown to Downtown, a lunch, two meetings and a drop at the airport is one booking rather than five, and the car and the chauffeur stay with you between stops instead of being re-dispatched.

Every vehicle class books by the hour with a three hour minimum. See hourly rates by class. The full hourly terms are set out under NYC chauffeur service.

  • MinimumThree hours on every hourly booking
  • Multi stopThe car waits between stops
  • On boardHigh-speed Wi-Fi and charging ports in the executive fleet

Corporate accounts

Corporate accounts with monthly billing

Corporate accounts carry monthly billing, priority scheduling and dedicated support. An assistant booking six rides a week is not re-entering a card six times, and the rides land on one invoice. The vendor checks a travel team runs are in a corporate black car checklist.

New clients may be asked to complete a credit card authorization form before the first reservation is confirmed. That is a fraud control and it happens once, at the start of the relationship.

  • BillingMonthly invoicing instead of payment per ride
  • SchedulingPriority scheduling for account holders
  • SetupOne card authorization form before the first ride

Events

Weddings and events

Event work is a movement plan more than a ride. Ceremony, reception and after party each need a vehicle in position before the guests are, which is what an hourly booking is for.

The two Luxury Sprinters seat 12 and take 10 large plus 2 small bags, so a wedding party travels together rather than in four separate cars. Sprinters book by the hour with the same three hour minimum. Sprinter seating and event planning are covered under limousine service.

  • Party sizeUp to 12 passengers per Sprinter
  • StagingHourly booking keeps the car with the party between locations
  • Luggage10 large plus 2 small bags per Sprinter

Prom

Prom transportation

Prom is booked by parents and ridden by teenagers, so the details that matter are the ones a parent asks about. A fixed pickup time, a professional chauffeur, and one vehicle that holds the whole group.

Smoking is prohibited in every vehicle. The two Sprinter classes carry 12 passengers each. Booking at least 24 hours ahead secures both the vehicle class and the time slot. Prom groups are handled through the same Sprinter classes.

  • GroupUp to 12 passengers in the Sprinter classes
  • PolicySmoking is prohibited in every vehicle
  • BookingAt least 24 hours ahead is recommended

Cruise terminals

Cruise terminal transfers

A cruise day moves a lot of luggage against a fixed deadline. Manhattan and Brooklyn embarkation both reward an early pickup window and a vehicle chosen for bag capacity rather than seat count. Brooklyn sailings leave from Pier 12 in Red Hook, which has no subway station.

Business SUV takes 3 large plus 3 small bags for up to 5 passengers. A Luxury Sprinter takes 10 large plus 2 small for up to 12. Either can be booked hourly when the day includes stops before the terminal. Passenger and luggage capacity is listed for all twelve vehicles.

  • TerminalsManhattan and Brooklyn embarkation
  • CapacityUp to 10 large bags in the Luxury Sprinter class
  • TimingEarly pickup windows on embarkation day
